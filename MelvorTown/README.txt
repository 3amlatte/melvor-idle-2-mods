MELVOR TOWN 0.1.0
for Melvor Idle 2 (tested on 0.11.7-alpha), needs Melvor Mod Loader 0.2.0+

A small medieval town that grows beside your adventures. A new Town tab appears
next to Bank in the top navigation bar.

  - Population and food: villagers arrive while there is food and room, and eat
    while you play. The town keeps running while the page is closed and catches
    up on time spent offline (up to 8 hours).
  - Food comes from farms (carrots and chickens), tree groves and merchants.
    Seeds keep the farms producing; the seed store trades food for seeds.
  - Upgrades need a population size and stored food. The Upgrades window lists
    every building and its requirement.
  - The town grows as you build: new sprites appear and the buildings get a
    little smaller to fit.
  - Click the farm, seed store, merchant, grove, houses, school or balloon to
    open its window. The tavern is scenery.
  - The cog in the top right opens settings (food rate, pause, reset).
  - Progress is saved to town.json in the mod folder.

INSTALL
  Extract the zip into the Melvor Idle 2 game folder so it ends up as
  mods\MelvorTown\MelvorTown.dll with the art folder beside it.
