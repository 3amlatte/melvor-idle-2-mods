PASSIVE SMELTING 0.1.1
for Melvor Idle 2 (tested on 0.11.6-alpha.1), needs Melvor Mod Loader 0.2.0+

The smelting recipe selected on the Smithing page keeps running while your character trains
another skill, and during offline progress. It never takes the active action slot.

BARS AT A TIME
  Smithing  1-50   1 bar at a time   (normal speed)
  Smithing 51-85   2 bars at a time  (interval halved)
  Smithing 86-99   3 bars at a time  (interval one third)

  The speed tiers shorten the smelting interval. Ore cost, XP and mastery stay exactly as the
  game intends for each bar; there are no free bars.

HOW TO USE
  1. Open Smithing and select a recipe in a smelting category (the bar recipes).
  2. That is all. The recipe starts on its own and keeps going when you switch to Woodcutting,
     Combat, Fishing or anything else.
  3. Change the recipe on the Smithing page to smelt something else. Selecting a non-smelting
     recipe stops the passive smelting.

  The Smithing milestones window has a "Passive Smelting" entry below the categories. It shows
  your current tier, the selected recipe and the full level table.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - The game gives Smithing a single action, so only one smelting recipe runs at a time.
    The tiers are speed, not extra recipes.
  - Passive smelting ticks during offline progress through the game's own Smithing component.
  - This is a cheat: it removes the game's one-action-at-a-time limit for smelting.
  - Nothing is written to the save. The mod starts the passive action again on every load.

INSTALL
  Put this folder under the game's mods folder, so the file ends up as
  mods\PassiveSmelting\PassiveSmelting.dll
  (Steam > Melvor Idle 2 > Manage > Browse local files)

UNINSTALL
  Delete the folder mods\PassiveSmelting
